About Me

I am a Computer Science Ph.D. candidate researching how to build fairness into intelligent systems. My work bridges natural language processing and recommendation systems, focusing on novel techniques to detect and mitigate algorithmic bias.

I hold a Master's degree in Information Systems and Software Engineering from the University of Bouira, which equipped me with a strong engineering foundation for designing complex systems"

When I am not teaching or researching, I actively explore the cutting edge of technology. My personal curiosity is particularly focused on the rapid evolution of Large Language Models and their expanding capabilities.
